*With Kimberly Huyser, Tamara Chavez, and Mary Jessome.*

Read about the talk on the [Dalla Lana School of Public Health website](https://www.dlsph.utoronto.ca/event/ciedar-overview-indigenous-voices-in-health-research/){:target="_blank"}.

**From the talk description:**
>CIEDAR (CoVaRR-Net’s Indigenous Engagement, Development, and Research Pillar 7) is an Indigenous and woman-led group of researchers, public health workers, and community members across Turtle Island. CIEDAR is passionate about seeing Indigenous communities thrive during current and future pandemics by hearing their voices. CIEDAR’s mission is to create research which helps Indigenous communities worldwide prepare for future pandemics.
