**Instructor:** Tom Einhorn

A workshop developed for master's students introducing the foundations of research ethics in the social sciences. The session covered the Tri-Council Policy Statement (TCPS2), ethical considerations in research involving Indigenous communities (including the OCAP® principles), and practical case study activities designed to help students navigate common ethical questions in their own research.

## Workshop Details

**Duration:** 3 hours.

**Participants:** Master's students at the Vancouver School of Theology
